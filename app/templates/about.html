My Story

How I got here

Before

I trained as an audio engineer, working with musicians, mixing sessions, and short film productions. I was good at the technical side — precision, clean output, working under deadline pressure. But I kept hitting a ceiling. The work wasn't giving me the kind of complexity I was looking for.

Pivot

Python & Algorithms Training

Code did it. I found Python and something clicked in a way audio engineering never quite had. The structure met the creativity, and it matched how I actually think. Level up

Went deep: AI and machine learning, data science, SQL — on top of coding three to four hours every day. 750+ hours of structured training across the full stack.

Where I am now

I write Python. Specifically: Flask web applications, SQL databases, data analysis with Pandas, and predictive models with scikit-learn. Over the past year I've put in 750+ hours of structured training across Python, AI, data science, and SQL — on top of coding three to four hours every day.

How I got here

I didn't start in tech. I trained as an audio engineer at SAE Institute Madrid, working with musicians, mixing sessions, and short film productions. I was good at the technical side — precision, clean output, working under deadline pressure. But I kept hitting a ceiling. The work wasn't giving me the kind of complexity I was looking for.

Code did. I found Python and something clicked in a way audio engineering never quite had. The structure met the creativity, and it matched how I actually think. The transition wasn't casual — I went from SAE to formal Python and algorithms training, then AI and machine learning, data science, SQL, and now Day 83 of 100 Days of Code.

What the audio world gave me has carried over directly: precision, the ability to iterate under pressure, and an intolerance for sloppy output.
